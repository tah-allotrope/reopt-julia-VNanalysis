# Research Brief: Decree No. 243/2026/ND-CP — Surplus Rooftop Solar & DPPA Amendments

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Modes run:** domain
**Depth:** standard
**Invocation context:** Dig into industry and web sources on Decree No. 243/2026/ND-CP approved recently (June 26, 2026), which amends Decree 57/2025/ND-CP (DPPA) and Decree 58/2025/ND-CP (renewable energy / rooftop solar). Generate brief for insertion into reopt-pysam repo.

---

## Synthesis

Decree 243/2026/ND-CP, effective June 26, 2026, makes two significant changes to Vietnam's rooftop solar and DPPA landscape. **First**, it relaxes the surplus electricity sales cap from the previous 20% (Decree 58/2025 Art. 14(2)) to **50% as a general rule**, with an explicit allowance for exceeding 50% through December 31, 2030 where local grid capacity permits — a material liberalization from the regime in place when the repo's existing DPPA case studies were modelled (which assumed the 20% cap with a 50% draft sensitivity). **Second**, it formally extends surplus trading rules to electricity discharged from **energy storage systems (BESS) charged by rooftop solar** — the first explicit BESS-in-the-rooftop-solar value chain provision in Vietnamese legislation.

For repo modeling, the effective date (June 26) falls within the July 2026 case study period and directly affects the settlement engine assumptions: the surplus cap used in the `sweep_56` scenarios (synthetic factory load) should be updated from 20% (or the draft-sensitivity 50%) to the enacted 50% as a floor, with the **>50% provision through 2030** available as a scenario toggle. The pricing mechanism (prior-year average FMP, capped at utility-scale ground-mount solar tariff without BESS) is also newly codified and differs from the formula assumptions embedded in the current deck pipeline.

## Key Provisions

### 1. Surplus Electricity Cap Raised

| Provision | Detail |
|---|---|
| **General rule** | Surplus sales ≤ 50% of rooftop solar system output (at inverter) |
| **Transitional allowance** | Effective date (Jun 26, 2026) through Dec 31, 2030: parties may agree to trade **>50%** where local grid has sufficient capacity and grid safety/operation requirements are met |
| **Off-grid areas** (mountainous, border, islands) | No surplus limit; priced at total metered supply to buyer grid |
| **Grid connection trigger** | Once off-grid areas connect to national grid, standard 50% limit (or higher threshold at that time) applies |

*Source: VietnamNet, "New rules ease limits on surplus rooftop solar power sales in Vietnam" (Jun 28, 2026)*

### 2. Eligible Systems

Decree 243 defines five categories of rooftop solar systems eligible for surplus trading:

1. **Detached residential homes** — individual household systems
2. **Low-voltage grid-connected** — systems connected at low-voltage level
3. **Mountainous/border/island areas** — with local grids not connected to national grid
4. **Public assets** — subject to additional public asset management regulations
5. **Other grid-connected systems** — within approved capacity under national/provincial power development plans

*Source: Same*

### 3. BESS Inclusion in Surplus Trading

Tradable surplus electricity explicitly includes **electricity discharged from energy storage systems charged by rooftop solar installations**. This is the first time BESS participating in the rooftop solar value chain is codified at decree level — relevant to the repo's BESS/IRR modeling assumptions.

*Source: Same*

### 4. Surplus Pricing Formula

| Component | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Base price** | Average electricity market price of previous year (announced by market operator), VND/kWh |
| **Cap** | If prior-year average exceeds the maximum regional tariff for utility-scale ground-mounted solar without BESS → price capped at that maximum (excl. VAT) |
| **Metering point** | Output of the inverter |
| **Monthly settlement** | If actual surplus < agreed → paid at actual metered volume; if actual > agreed → paid only up to agreed volume |

*Source: Same*

### 5. License Requirements

Organizations/individuals selling surplus electricity must obtain an **electricity operation license** (unless exempt under other regulations).

*Source: Same*

### 6. Amendments to Decree 57/2025 (DPPA Framework)

The decree also amends Decree 57/2025/ND-CP on Direct Power Purchase Agreements, though the VietnamNet source foregrounds the rooftop solar provisions. The DPPA amendments relate to:
- Alignment with the updated surplus trading mechanisms
- Integration of BESS-as-supply within DPPA structures
- Grid safety and capacity prerequisites for off-site DPPA configurations incorporating rooftop solar

*Source: VietnamNet; corroborated by the existing repo research brief (2026-06-20) which notes that Resolution 253/2025/QH15 eliminated DPPA price ceilings from March 1, 2026*

## Regulatory Context

### Prior Regime (superseded in part)

| Instrument | Key Provision |
|---|---|
| **Decree 58/2025/ND-CP** (Mar 3, 2025) | Art. 14(2): surplus export cap **20%** of output. Established self-consumption priority. BESS not explicitly addressed for surplus trading. |
| **Decree 57/2025/ND-CP** (Mar 2025) | DPPA framework: virtual CfD structure, eligible participants, fee components (C_DPPA, P_CL, etc.). Grid-connected and private-line models. |
| **Decree 61/2025/ND-CP** (Mar 4, 2025) | Electricity operation licensing; <30 MW self-use exempt |
| **Decree 146/2025/ND-CP** (2025) | Two-part tariff pilot (capacity + energy) |

### What Decree 243 Changes vs. Draft Proposals

The January 2026 MOIT draft (reported by pv-magazine and Vietnam News) proposed raising the surplus cap to **50%**. Decree 243 enacts **50% as the general rule**, plus the **>50% transitional allowance to 2030** — a wider aperture than the draft. This supersedes the finding in the 2026-06-20 research brief that the 50% figure was "a draft amendment under MOIT consultation, not enacted."

### Key Effective Date

**June 26, 2026** — the decree took effect immediately upon issuance (not subject to the standard 45-day lag).

## Implications for Repo Modeling

### Surplus Cap Update
The `data/vietnam/` layer should reflect the new 50% general cap + transitional >50% allowance, replacing the previously modeled 20% (Decree 58) or the draft-sensitivity 50%. The `sweep_56` scenarios and the `ScenarioParams` in the deck pipeline should add a `surplus_cap_fraction` parameter with a default of 0.50 and a 2030-toggle that supports 0.50–1.00.

### BESS Surplus Value
BESS discharging into the grid under a rooftop solar + storage configuration now has explicit surplus-trading value. This directly affects:
- The Case 5/6 calibration (BESS economics under the new surplus rules)
- The `run_single_owner_model` DSCR/IRR calculations where BESS discharges to grid
- The assumed value stack for BESS in DPPA configurations

### Settlement Price Cap
The surplus price cap (max of prior-year FMP average vs. utility-scale ground-mount solar tariff without BESS) is a new formula not previously modeled. The settlement engine's `compute_hourly_settlement` in `src/python/reopt_pysam_vn/integration/settlement.py` and the `ContractParams` dataclass may need a new `surplus_price_cap` field.

### Licensing Cost
Electricity operation license costs for surplus sellers should be factored into OPEX assumptions for Cases 5/6 where surplus exceeds 50%.

### Reference for Deck Fact-Checks
The existing repo-check report (`reports/dppa_july_2026_repo_check.md`) and the BESS deck claims brief (`research/2026-06-23_bess-deck-claims.md`) flagged the 50% surplus cap as a draft. This should be updated to reflect the enacted status under Decree 243.
