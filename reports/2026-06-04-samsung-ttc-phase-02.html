05 / Results & Visualizations

Results & Visualizations

On the contracted 70 GWh, the buyer's effective cost (strike 1,012 + DPPA grid-service adder 523 + small KPP loss) sits at 1,553 VND/kWh — well under the 1,916 VND/kWh the buyer would otherwise pay EVN for that solar-hour energy. The DPPA grid-service adder (inherited from the Case-2 default) consumes the largest share of the gross headroom and is the single most impactful assumption to vary in PHASE-03/04. All figures directional.

06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • Solar is a proxy. PySAM PVWatts was unavailable, so the 8,760 is a representative southern profile calibrated to 70 GWh — not a Duc Hue 2 site-specific yield. Second-best (when PySAM is installed): run PVWatts at the Tay Ninh coordinates and re-calibrate.
  • Inherited adder & KPP. The 523.34 VND/kWh DPPA grid-service adder and 1.0273 KPP come from the Case-2 ninhsim defaults; they materially shape the buyer's effective cost and should be deal-calibrated.
  • Synthetic buyer load. Savings are computed on the matched slice, so the load shape barely matters here — but the 1,000 GWh total remains an assumption.
  • Single strike point. This phase reports the base strike (1,012). The full buyer-premium surface and developer viability come in PHASE-03.
