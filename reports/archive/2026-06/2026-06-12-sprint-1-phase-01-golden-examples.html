06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • Limitation: Only three runs are frozen. They cover one onsite REopt solve and one offsite/DPPA case (Samsung-TTC) — not every case study (ninhsim, north_thuan, saigon18 DPPA cases 1-3).
  • Why acceptable: The goal is a representative reference, not a full archive; git history still retains every untracked artifact, and the scripts can regenerate any of them.
  • Second-best alternative: Force-add (git add -f) the originals in place instead of copying to examples/. Rejected — it would keep the bloated paths tracked and defeat Phase 02's untracking.
