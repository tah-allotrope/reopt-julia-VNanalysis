06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • Developer revenue is conservative. It is taken on the contracted 70 GWh, but the real 49 MWp plant generates ~92.5 GWh (sunny South); counting full output + merchant tail would lift developer NPV materially. The "sub-economic" finding is therefore a floor, not a verdict.
  • Null IRR is expected. The repo's Single Owner setup returns null IRR when the cashflow never crosses positive (same as Case-2 Phase F); NPV is the usable developer metric here.
  • Inherited adder dominates. The DPPA grid-service adder (523.34) is a Case-2 default, not deal-calibrated, yet it sets the sign of the buyer result — the single most important number to source for real.
  • Capex assumed. $750/kW is a market estimate; lower capex (utility solar is falling) would improve developer NPV.
