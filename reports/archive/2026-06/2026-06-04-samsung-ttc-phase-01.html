06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • Directional only. Strike, tenor, KPP/grid fee are all undisclosed. The 1,012 base strike and the CFMP proxy drive every downstream number; treat as directional, not bankable.
  • Synthetic buyer load. SEVT's real 8,760 and total consumption are not public; the ~1,000 GWh megafactory profile is an assumption sized so the 70 GWh is fully matched. Second-best: ingest a real SEVT interval series if one becomes available.
  • Voltage defaulted. No public confirmation of SEVT's connection voltage; 110 kV used (220 kV plausible at >100 MW scale; rate delta ~1%).
  • Second-best lane: a Julia REopt solve with pinned sizing would give REopt-native hourly flows, but adds cold-start minutes; Phase 01 chose the Python-only lane per Q3.
