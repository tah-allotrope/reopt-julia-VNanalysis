Samsung SEVT ↔ TTC Duc Hue 2 DPPA — Economics

A repo-grounded, directional economic model of Vietnam's first grid-connected Direct Power Purchase Agreement (live 1 June 2026), built on the reopt-pysam-vn synthetic-DPPA settlement engine and PySAM. Project: Samsung-TTC DPPA Economics · Repo: reopt-pysam · Date: 2026-06-04.

Bottom line (directional): at a strike anchored to the Southern solar ceiling (1,012 VND/kWh), Samsung saves ~25 B VND/yr (~$0.95M) on the contracted 70 GWh, but the developer is sub-economic across the regulated strike band under conservative assumptions — so there is no clean buyer–developer overlap. The deal works for the offtaker; the generator's return depends on counting the plant's full yield and a deal-calibrated grid fee.

  • Buyer (Samsung SEVT): the DPPA undercuts EVN. Effective cost on the contracted 70 GWh is ~1,552 VND/kWh vs ~1,913 EVN avoided cost — a ~25 B VND/yr saving. The buyer stays favourable up to a strike of ~1,440 VND/kWh, then flips to a premium.
  • Developer (TTC): at any strike inside the Decree-57 ceiling band, the Single Owner model returns negative NPV and null IRR — but on a conservative basis (revenue counted only on the contracted 70 GWh, not the plant's full ~92.5 GWh yield, at an assumed $750/kW capex).   • Dominant lever: the DPPA grid-service adder (inherited 523 VND/kWh) sets the sign of the buyer's result — break-even near 0.9×. Sourcing the real wheeling/grid fee is the single highest-value next input.
  • Regulatory robustness: the Decree 146 two-part tariff trial lifts Samsung's EVN bill ~18%, which would make the DPPA more attractive — unless it double-charges the contracted volume (a known risk).

05Recommendation & Path to Bankable

Directional position: buyer-favourable, developer sub-economic — no clean overlap.

  • For the offtaker: the deal structurally beats EVN at the regulated ceiling strike; the saving is modest (~$1M/yr on a 70 GWh slice of a ~$80M/yr power bill) but real, and improves if the two-part tariff arrives.
  • For the generator: do not conclude "uneconomic" from this screen — it is conservative. Re-run counting the plant's full ~92.5 GWh output plus a merchant tail on the uncontracted ~22 GWh, and test lower capex ($/kW is falling).
  • Three inputs move this from directional to bankable: (1) the real DPPA grid/wheeling fee, (2) a Tay Ninh site-specific CFMP/FMP series, (3) the actual negotiated strike and tenor.

06Risks & Caveats

  • Directional only. Strike, DPPA adder, KPP and tenor are undisclosed/triangulated; the CFMP series and solar resource are non-site-specific proxies.
  • Developer result is a floor. Conservative revenue/capex basis; null IRR is documented Single Owner behaviour, not necessarily project failure.
  • Two-part tariff double-charging. Decree 146 could apply the capacity charge to DPPA volume, eroding the buyer's gain.
