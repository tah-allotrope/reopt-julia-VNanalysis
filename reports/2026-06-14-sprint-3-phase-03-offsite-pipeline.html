06 / Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

  • Honest scope: a from-scratch generic orchestrator for a brand-new deal (one with no registered builder) needs a REopt/PySAM solve to produce the generation results the settlement engine consumes. That solver-dependent path is wired conceptually but deferred — run_offsite_dppa raises a clear, actionable error for an unregistered deal rather than faking a generic engine I can't verify to parity.
  • Second-best alternative: hand-roll a generic settlement builder now. Rejected — high risk of a subtly-wrong engine; routing to the proven, tested builder is correct and honest, and PHASE-04 will pin it to the golden.
