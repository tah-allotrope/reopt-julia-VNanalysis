Which volume?
You settle only on
what you actually consume
MODULE
2
-
THE FIVE-LINE BILL
Q_adj = Q_meter / K_pp        delivered volume at your meter
Q_Khc = min(Load, Q_adj)      settled volume (lines 1-3)
Q_CfD = min(Q_c, Q_Khc)       CfD settles on received volume
Grid losses are reflected through coordinated volume and price conversions:
K_pp converts generator-meter output into buyer-side credited volume, while
k and K_pp convert FMP into the corresponding buyer-side market-energy price.
If the plant generates more than you consume in an hour, the excess earns the generator spot revenue only
;
it is NOT billed to you and does NOT earn CfD
Practical consequence:
contracted volume (Q_c) above your real consumption buys nothing

Module 3:
CfD Mechanics

[Slide 13]
The CfD is two-way: certainty, not automatic savings
MODULE  -
CFD MECHANICS
CfD cash flow = (Strike - FMP) x Q_CfD
Market condition
Who pays whom
FMP below strike
You top up the generator
FMP above strike
Generator pays you back
Net effect:
the
renewable energy cost is locked near the strike (+ fees), whatever the market does
2025 reference: FMP averaged ~1,427 VND/kWh with strong hourly volatility - the CfD removes that volatility from
the
budget
A strike below expected average FMP would mean expected receipts - but no developer can finance that (Module 4)
